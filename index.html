Frequently Asked Questions

Good Questions Deserve Straight Answers.

Here are a few places to start. Every situation is different, so I'm happy to talk through yours.

Do I need to be ready to buy or sell before contacting you?

Not at all. Whether your plans are for next month or next year, we can talk through your options and a timeline that works for you.

Can you help me understand my home's value?

Yes. Start with the home value request, and we can discuss comparable properties, condition, and market factors that may influence a potential sale price.

Do you work with VA homebuyers?

Yes. As a Navy veteran, I understand why the details matter. I can help you navigate the real estate side of a VA purchase and connect you with a qualified lender for loan-specific questions.

What if I'm relocating to the Dallas–Fort Worth area?

We can begin with your commute, lifestyle, budget, and priorities, then explore communities that fit. You don't have to know the area before reaching out.

How does Homes for Heroes work?

The program supports eligible community heroes. Visit my Homes for Heroes registration page to review current eligibility and benefits.
