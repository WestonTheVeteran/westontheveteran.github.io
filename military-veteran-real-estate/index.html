Buying a home with a VA loan

Eligible service members, veterans and certain surviving spouses may qualify for a VA-backed purchase loan. A Certificate of Eligibility (COE) establishes eligibility for the benefit, not automatic mortgage approval. A lender evaluates income, credit, entitlement and the property.

  1. Obtain your COE and speak with a VA-experienced lender.
  2. Compare loan estimates, monthly payments and cash needed at closing.
  3. Search for homes that fit your budget, commute and needs.
  4. Arrange an independent inspection and coordinate the appraisal and closing.

Zero down may be available, but closing costs, prepaid expenses and a funding fee may apply. Some borrowers qualify for a funding fee exemption.

Planning a PCS move to North Texas

Start with your reporting date and work backward. For moves involving NAS JRB Fort Worth or other DFW assignments, compare actual commute routes and schedules before choosing a location.

  • Confirm orders, travel dates and temporary housing options.
  • Compare renting and buying based on expected assignment length and total costs.
  • Plan remote tours, inspections and closing logistics if you are out of state.
  • Keep a contingency plan for changing orders or delayed closing.

Understanding VA assumable mortgages

An approved assumption may allow a buyer to take over an existing VA loan's remaining balance, rate and terms. The servicer must confirm eligibility and approval requirements. A buyer may need cash or approved additional financing to cover the difference between the purchase price and remaining loan balance.

For example, a hypothetical $350,000 purchase with a $280,000 remaining loan balance leaves a $70,000 price gap before closing costs. A lower interest rate alone does not establish affordability.

Sellers should confirm release of liability and whether VA entitlement can be restored or substituted. Non-veterans may be eligible to assume certain VA loans, but entitlement consequences for the seller require careful review.

Selling a home during a military move

Start with a local market analysis, estimated net proceeds and a realistic timeline. Consider repairs, mortgage payoff, closing costs and your next housing arrangement. If your current loan is assumable, ask the servicer about the process before advertising that option.

Military real estate frequently asked questions

Who can help me buy a home with a VA loan in DFW?

Weston Davis is a Navy veteran and REALTOR® with On Demand Realty serving North Texas. He can help coordinate your home search and transaction with your chosen lender.

Can I use a VA loan more than once?

Potentially, depending on remaining or restored entitlement and lender approval.

Does a VA appraisal replace a home inspection?

No. An independent inspection evaluates condition separately from the lender's appraisal.

Can I buy remotely before a PCS move?

Remote tours and coordinated inspections may help. Confirm signing, occupancy and closing arrangements with your lender and title company.
