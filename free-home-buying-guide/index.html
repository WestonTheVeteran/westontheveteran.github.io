Before You Buy a Home, Know What It Could Really Cost You.

A mortgage payment is only part of the picture. Taxes, insurance, closing costs, and the wrong financing assumptions can change your budget fast. Get a practical guide to help you plan before you make an offer.

What You’ll Get in Your Free Guide

  • Estimate your real monthly payment: look beyond principal and interest to taxes, insurance, and HOA dues.
  • Plan for cash to close: organize down payment, closing costs, prepaids, and credits.
  • Know your buying power: use budget and debt-to-income worksheets to prepare for a lender conversation.
  • Compare renting versus buying: understand the monthly tradeoffs before deciding.
  • Navigate the process: follow a step-by-step buying roadmap and property-tour checklist.
  • Buying with a VA loan? Review key considerations for eligible military and veteran buyers.
