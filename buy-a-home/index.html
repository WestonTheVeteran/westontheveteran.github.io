Your Home Buying Roadmap

1. Set priorities

Consider budget, commute, community, and lifestyle.

2. Explore financing

Speak with a qualified lender about preapproval, costs, and loan options.

3. Tour homes

Compare condition, location, features, and long-term fit.

4. Make an offer

Review price, contingencies, timelines, and negotiation strategy.

5. Evaluate the property

Discuss inspection, appraisal, insurance, and contract deadlines.

6. Close and move

Understand final figures, closing, and possession.
