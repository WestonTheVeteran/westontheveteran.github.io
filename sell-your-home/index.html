Before You List: Four Questions Worth Asking

What is my home worth today?

An informed estimate considers recent comparable sales and today's competing properties—not just an automated online valuation.

What should I fix first?

Some repairs or updates may improve marketability; others may not justify the cost. Prioritize based on your situation.

How long might selling take?

Timing varies by price, condition, location, buyer demand, and contract terms. We'll discuss a realistic range instead of promising a deadline.

What will I net from the sale?

Review estimated mortgage payoff, selling expenses, concessions, and other potential costs before deciding on your next step.

Before You List

A Practical Seller Preparation Checklist

Make a strong first impression

Tidy landscaping, declutter, deep clean, and address obvious maintenance items where practical.

Gather important information

Collect information about improvements, warranties, HOA requirements, and known property conditions. Discuss applicable seller disclosures.

Decide what is worth doing

Not every renovation pays for itself. We can discuss repairs and presentation based on your timeline and likely buyer expectations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to renovate before selling?

Not necessarily. We can review your home's condition and likely buyer expectations before you spend money on improvements.

Can I sell while buying another home?

Yes. The best approach depends on financing, timing, and your comfort with overlapping transactions. We can map out the options.
