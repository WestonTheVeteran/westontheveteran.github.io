8. Your Home Buying Roadmap

  1. Clarify your budget and priorities.
  2. Discuss preapproval and financing with a qualified lender.
  3. Search homes and compare neighborhoods.
  4. Review representation terms and negotiate an offer.
  5. Evaluate inspection, appraisal, insurance and contract deadlines.
  6. Review closing figures, verify wire instructions independently, and plan your move.

VA buyers: Eligible borrowers may qualify for zero down; other costs, lender approval, funding fees (unless exempt), and property requirements may apply. A VA appraisal does not replace an inspection.
